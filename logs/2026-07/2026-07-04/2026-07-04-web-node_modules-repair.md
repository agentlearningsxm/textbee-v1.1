## Lesson
- pnpm virtual-store repair on Windows: do NOT delete a single `.pnpm/<pkg>` folder — it strips the
  sibling dependency junctions (styled-jsx etc.) nested inside and leaves the store half-wired.
  For a corrupt package, remove the whole `node_modules` and reinstall (store stays intact, relink is
  from disk, no re-download). Surgical single-package deletion caused a second failure.
